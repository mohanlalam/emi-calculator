# 📱 EMI & Finance Calculator (iOS PWA)

A fast, privacy-focused, and modern financial calculator designed with a native iOS look & feel. Works 100% offline and can be installed directly to your iPhone or iPad Home Screen via Safari.

🌐 **Live GitHub Pages URL**: [https://mohanlalam.github.io/emi-calculator/](https://mohanlalam.github.io/emi-calculator/)

---

## ✨ Features

- **🏠 Smart Loan EMI Calculator**:
  - Home, Car, and Personal loan presets.
  - Interactive Donut chart displaying Principal vs. Interest percentage.
  - Full amortization schedule (Yearly & Monthly breakdown).
  - ⚡ **Prepayment & Extra EMI Simulator**: See exactly how much interest and tenure you save with extra monthly or lump-sum payments.
  - Quick-preset chips (₹10L, ₹25L, ₹50L, ₹1Cr; 5Y, 10Y, 15Y, 20Y).
- **📈 SIP Wealth Growth Calculator**:
  - SIP projection with wealth multiplier.
  - Optional Annual Step-Up percentage.
  - Year-by-year invested vs. returns progression table.
- **🏦 Side-by-Side Loan Comparison**:
  - Compare Home Loan vs. Car Loan vs. Personal Loan rates and monthly outlays side-by-side.
  - Highlights lowest EMI and least interest burden.
- **💼 Investment Suite**:
  - Compare All (SIP vs. Lumpsum vs. FD vs. RD vs. PPF vs. Debt Funds).
  - Lumpsum Calculator with inflation-adjusted real purchasing power.
  - Fixed Deposit (FD) Calculator with customizable compounding frequency (Monthly, Quarterly, Half-Yearly, Annual).
  - Recurring Deposit (RD) Calculator.
- **⚖️ Stock & Crypto Average Calculator**:
  - Multi-tranche buy averaging with portfolio weightings.
  - Real-time break-even price and live P&L tracking at Current Market Price (CMP).
- **📱 iOS Native PWA Experience**:
  - iOS bottom tab bar for easy one-handed navigation.
  - Safe-area support for iPhone notch & Dynamic Island.
  - Dark Mode & Light Mode (OLED true blacks).
  - Web Share API (share loan quotes via WhatsApp, Messages, or clipboard).
  - CSV Export & Print/PDF report generation.
  - 100% offline support using Service Workers (`sw.js`).

## 📲 How to Install on iOS (iPhone & iPad)

1. Open **[https://mohanlalam.github.io/emi-calculator/](https://mohanlalam.github.io/emi-calculator/)** in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an upward arrow `⎋`) in Safari's bottom toolbar.
3. Scroll down and select **"Add to Home Screen"** (`➕`).
4. Tap **"Add"** in the top-right corner.
5. The **EMI Calc** icon will now appear on your home screen and run full-screen just like a native App Store app!
